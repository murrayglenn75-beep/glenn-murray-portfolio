## Executive Summary
The Glenn Method is an AI-assisted project-scoping system for structured GO / NO-GO / CAUTION recommendations, complexity analysis, executive proposals, pricing guidance, and commercial decision support.
## Business Problem
Commercial decisions need clear scope, complexity, risk, and value framing before teams commit to delivery.
## Requirements
The system requires structured recommendations, complexity analysis, executive communication, pricing guidance, and explicit business-value framing.
## Architecture
The method structures project inputs into decision intelligence, complexity assessment, proposal generation, and executive-ready outputs.
## Technology Stack
Commercial scoping, decision intelligence, complexity assessment, executive communication, business-value framing, and proposal generation.
## Engineering Decisions
Recommendations are framed as structured decision support rather than autonomous commercial decisions.
## AI Usage Boundaries
AI assists reasoning and communication but does not own GO / NO-GO decisions, commercial accountability, or final pricing judgement.
## Challenges
The challenge is preserving useful structure while recognising that commercial context and accountability remain human responsibilities.
## Testing
Testing focuses on whether outputs remain structured, traceable to supplied context, and suitable for review.
## Validation
Validation uses acceptance criteria for recommendation structure, decision framing, and completeness of commercial context.
## Deployment Status
No public deployment status is claimed.
## Lessons Learned
Clear framing and explicit decision boundaries create more value than unstructured generative output.
## Future Improvements
Future improvements can extend controlled input schemas and review workflows without changing decision ownership.
