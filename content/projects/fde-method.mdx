## Executive Summary
FDE Method is a structured multi-stage engagement methodology for AI projects, from readiness assessment through architecture, validation, proof of concept, and production rollout.
## Business Problem
AI initiatives often begin before readiness, requirements, data, and quality gates have been made explicit.
## Requirements
The method requires requirements decomposition, data audits, architecture, pipeline design, quality gates, proof of concept, validation, and production delivery.
## Architecture
The methodology structures engagements into readiness assessment, technical architecture, pipeline design, validation, and staged rollout.
## Technology Stack
Requirements decomposition, data audits, architecture, pipeline design, quality gates, proof of concept, validation, and production delivery.
## Engineering Decisions
Each stage is designed to expose uncertainty early and avoid progressing to the next stage without suitable evidence.
## AI Usage Boundaries
AI can accelerate exploration and implementation, but readiness, architecture, validation, and rollout decisions remain engineering-led.
## Challenges
The challenge is sequencing work so that business readiness and technical validation are established before production commitments.
## Testing
Quality gates and proof-of-concept validation provide controlled testing points throughout the engagement.
## Validation
Validation is a distinct stage rather than an assumed consequence of a successful prototype.
## Deployment Status
No public deployment status is claimed.
## Lessons Learned
Forward deployment is a disciplined delivery model, not simply a job title or a rapid prototype exercise.
## Future Improvements
The method can evolve through better readiness criteria and reusable quality-gate templates.
