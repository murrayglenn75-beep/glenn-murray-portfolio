## Executive Summary
AXO Engine is an enterprise sustainability platform for emissions data, governance, compliance, supply-chain intelligence, explainable AI, telemetry, and human review.
## Business Problem
Sustainability decisions require connected operational, compliance, and supply-chain context rather than isolated reporting outputs.
## Requirements
The system requires governance, compliance context, carbon-accounting intelligence, explainability, telemetry, and human review.
## Architecture
The architecture connects knowledge-graph concepts, emissions data, supply-chain intelligence, explainability, telemetry, and human review.
## Technology Stack
Knowledge graphs, carbon accounting, compliance, governance, explainable AI, telemetry, and human review.
## Engineering Decisions
Human review and explainability are treated as core system capabilities rather than optional reporting features.
## AI Usage Boundaries
AI-assisted insight must remain explainable and subject to human review; it is not positioned as an autonomous compliance authority.
## Challenges
The central challenge is maintaining useful intelligence across governance, compliance, carbon-accounting, and supply-chain concerns.
## Testing
Regression testing is a stated system capability for protecting expected behavior as the platform evolves.
## Validation
Validation focuses on explainability, governance context, human review, and regression coverage rather than unsupported performance claims.
## Deployment Status
No public deployment status is claimed.
## Lessons Learned
Enterprise intelligence benefits from connecting domain context, review, and explainability before adding automation.
## Future Improvements
Future improvements should preserve governance, explainability, and human review as the platform expands.
