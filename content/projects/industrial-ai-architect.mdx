## Executive Summary
Industrial AI Architect is an AI platform supporting manufacturing optimisation, operational intelligence, engineering analysis, and industrial decision support.
## Business Problem
Industrial operations need context-aware support across manufacturing, engineering analysis, and operational improvement.
## Requirements
The platform requires manufacturing context, operational intelligence, engineering analysis, industrial optimisation, and decision support.
## Architecture
The system is framed around industrial-engineering intelligence connecting manufacturing, operations, engineering analysis, and optimisation.
## Technology Stack
Manufacturing, operations, engineering intelligence, industrial optimisation, and decision support.
## Engineering Decisions
Industrial context and operational constraints are treated as primary design inputs rather than an afterthought to generic AI interaction.
## AI Usage Boundaries
AI supports analysis and decision support; it does not replace engineering judgement or fabricate operational facts.
## Challenges
The challenge is making AI useful within the constraints and failure modes of real industrial systems.
## Testing
Testing focuses on whether system outputs remain grounded in supplied operational and engineering context.
## Validation
Validation prioritises traceable operational reasoning and practical decision support.
## Deployment Status
No public deployment status is claimed.
## Lessons Learned
Industrial systems benefit from AI only when operational context and engineering constraints remain visible.
## Future Improvements
Future improvements should strengthen context, validation, and industrial decision-support workflows.
