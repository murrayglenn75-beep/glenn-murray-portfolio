## Executive Summary
CFO OS is an executive finance platform that treats deterministic reconciliation and trusted calculations as prerequisites for AI-generated insight.
## Business Problem
Executive reporting is not trustworthy when underlying reconciliations and calculations are uncertain.
## Requirements
The system requires financial reconciliation, confidence scoring, executive dashboards, deterministic calculations, and controlled LLM-assisted reporting.
## Architecture
The platform establishes trusted calculations and reconciliation before presenting executive dashboards and AI-assisted reporting.
## Technology Stack
Financial reconciliation, confidence scoring, executive dashboards, deterministic calculations, and LLM-assisted reporting.
## Engineering Decisions
Deterministic financial calculations are a prerequisite for any generative reporting layer.
## AI Usage Boundaries
AI can assist reporting over trusted calculations; it does not replace reconciliation or create unverified financial facts.
## Challenges
The challenge is preserving confidence and explainability from underlying calculations through executive-facing insight.
## Testing
Testing focuses on reconciliation and deterministic-calculation correctness before reporting behavior is evaluated.
## Validation
Validation prioritises trusted calculations and confidence scoring over unsupported generated narrative.
## Deployment Status
No public deployment status is claimed.
## Lessons Learned
In executive systems, reliable calculations must come before generative insight.
## Future Improvements
Future improvements should maintain the separation between reconciliation, confidence scoring, and AI-assisted reporting.
